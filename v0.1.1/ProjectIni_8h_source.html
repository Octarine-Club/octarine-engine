3// C++ reader for the game project's `project.ini` — third corner alongside the CMake parser
4// (`cmake/OctarinePackage.cmake :: octarine_read_project_ini`) and the Gradle parser
5// (`android/app/build.gradle :: java.util.Properties`). Used by the editor to surface project
6// identity in panels and to validate before Export Build spawns a packaging script.
7//
8// Schema matches the other two parsers; validation mirrors the shipping-build identity rules
9// (required: name, version_name, package_id; package_id must be reverse-DNS).
