13// Owns the off-screen scene target the game renders into each frame, plus SDL_RenderTarget
14// switching and presentation. Game::Render orchestrates the frame sequence (BeginScene, DrawQueue,
15// EndScene, CompositeSceneToWindow, Present).
16//
17// The scene texture is sized to the window at creation time. Lifetime tracks the SDL renderer:
18// CreateScene is called after the renderer is initialized, and DestroyScene before destruction.
19// Window resizing preserves the original render target size and relies on SDL letterboxing.
